What is BetterRankings?

Explore the playground

Changing how results are ranked usually means a slow, risky A/B test. BetterRankings answers “would this ranking do better?” in seconds, from clicks you already have.

Reuse old clicks

Your current ranking's log is reweighted to show what a different ranking would have earned.

Checked against the truth

A simulator knows the real answer, so you can see which estimators to trust.

Private by default

Runs in your browser. No server, no upload, no tracking.

Under the hood

How it works

If the current ranking shuffles a little and records how likely each list was, its old logs can answer a new question.

  1. 01

    Log

    The current ranking shows lists and records clicks, plus how likely each list was.

  2. 02

    Reweight

    weight = Pnew(list)Pold(list)

  3. 03

    Estimate

    Weighted clicks predict the new ranking's clicks, without showing it to anyone.
